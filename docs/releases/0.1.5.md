yapp 0.1.5 adds a first-launch tutorial, a live diagnostics log, and a lowercase name.

- New interactive tutorial on first launch (and for anyone updating who hasn't seen it): practice dictation in a centered box, with the real listening pill and callouts. Replay anytime from Advanced → Show Tutorial.
- Holding the shortcut while the model loads shows a preparation pill and starts recording when ready; releasing early cancels.
- Non-speech labels such as "(audience chattering)", "[Music]", or "*laughs*" are no longer inserted.
- Advanced → Diagnostics → Show Log opens a live table of this launch's diagnostic events, with Copy Last 5 Dictations for sharing. No audio, transcripts, or clipboard contents are included.
- The app is now named yapp. Updating keeps your settings and permissions.
- Simpler menus: Permissions shows Microphone and Accessibility with direct Settings links; refreshed About and menu-bar icon.

Dictation remains fully offline with the bundled English model. Updates remain manual through Check for Updates….
