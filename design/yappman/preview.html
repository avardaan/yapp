Yapp · character exploration

A little more our own.

New silhouettes, warmer orange, same quiet cheek. Moving away from the circle-and-wedge mouth. Each direction is shown as an app icon, in the floaty, and at smaller sizes.

01Little yapper

A soft, lopsided pebble with a wide little grin. Friendly without being loud.

02Chatterbox

A compact speech-box character. Strong silhouette, simple at tiny sizes.

03The little megaphone

A cheeky speaking profile: an oblong body and a short, rounded snout.

04Yip

A small upright character with a springy, unmistakable outline.

05Murmur

Two overlapping speech shapes. Quieter and more graphic than a mascot.

06Pip

A little orange with a single swept tuft and a sideways smile.

My starting points: 01 for a warm, expressive companion, 03 for a speaking character with a distinct profile, or 04 for a silhouette tied to Yapp’s name. We can refine the winning shape before animating it.
